About KeyboardBuilder

KeyboardBuilder is a specialized configuration tool crafted by Anaf. Designed with a minimalist, "Zinc" aesthetic, it serves as a bridge for enthusiasts to visualize and price their custom setups before purchasing.

Every mechanical keyboard is a unique reflection of its user. Our platform focuses on the three core pillars of the hobby: the Barebone kit, the Switches, and the Keycaps.
